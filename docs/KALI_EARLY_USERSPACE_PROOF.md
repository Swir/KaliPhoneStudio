# Kali early-userspace proof contract

This document defines how KaliPhoneStudio distinguishes **"the rescue initramfs ran"** from **"the exact reviewed Kali rootfs reached early systemd userspace"**.

It is deliberately a proof/evidence layer, not a rootfs transport implementation. A phone still needs a separately reviewed way to make the exact Kali rootfs available to the booted kernel. No storage path is assumed or hard-coded by the common core.

## Why a second marker is required

The rescue ramdisk marker proves only that the temporary-boot kernel entered the KaliPhoneStudio rescue initramfs. That is useful for console, logging and read-only diagnostics, but it does **not** prove that the kernel mounted or switched to the intended Kali ARM64 rootfs.

The early-userspace proof overlay therefore runs from the Kali rootfs itself as a deterministic systemd oneshot before `basic.target`. Seeing its complete marker set means the captured log contains signals generated by files whose identities are bound to the reviewed rootfs/candidate authority chain.

Even then, KaliPhoneStudio records an **observation requiring manual review**, not an automatic Beta pass.

## Host-side chain

`KaliEarlyUserspaceProbePlan` is bound to:

- the exact schema-v8 first-boot manifest digest exposed by the reviewed authority bundle;
- the exact reviewed kernel/rootfs/device-tree authority bundle digest;
- the exact rootfs-authority binding digest;
- the exact reviewed rootfs authority digest;
- the exact strict rootfs artifact SHA-256 and size;
- ARM64 architecture and rootfs variant.

The deterministic `probe_id` is SHA-256 over that canonical identity plus the proof policy. Any change to candidate, authority bundle, rootfs authority or rootfs artifact produces a different probe id.

The generated USTAR overlay has exactly five members:

1. `usr/share/kaliphonestudio/kali-early-userspace-probe-id`
2. `usr/share/kaliphonestudio/kali-early-userspace-plan.json`
3. `usr/libexec/kaliphonestudio/emit-early-userspace-proof`
4. `usr/lib/systemd/system/kaliphonestudio-early-userspace-proof.service`
5. `etc/systemd/system/basic.target.wants/kaliphonestudio-early-userspace-proof.service`

The tar member order, uid/gid, owner/group, mode and mtime are canonical and verified after build. The activation link is relative and fixed. The overlay contains no password, password hash, private key or remote-access credential and enables no SSH/network service.

## Runtime markers

When the exact probe unit executes from the Kali rootfs it emits these exact lines to standard output and, when writable, `/dev/console` and `/dev/kmsg`:

```text
KPS_KALI_STAGE=rootfs-systemd-early-v1
KPS_KALI_PROBE_ID=<64-hex probe id>
KPS_KALI_MANIFEST_SHA256=<64-hex first-boot manifest digest>
KPS_KALI_ROOTFS_AUTHORITY_SHA256=<64-hex reviewed rootfs authority digest>
KPS_KALI_ROOTFS_ARTIFACT_SHA256=<64-hex strict rootfs artifact digest>
```

The unit performs no Fastboot/ADB call, mounts no storage, changes no charging policy and writes no phone storage. Its only runtime output targets are the console/kernel log devices and normal process stdout.

## Physical observation contract

`PhysicalKaliEarlyUserspaceEvidence` accepts only:

- a previously successful, non-persistent `TemporaryBootExecutionEvidence`;
- a `PhysicalCandidateGateEvidence` bound to the same serial, candidate and reviewed authority bundle;
- the exact `KaliEarlyUserspaceProbeBundleEvidence` matching the gate's candidate/rootfs;
- an operator-captured, immutable transcript containing every exact marker above.

The parser rejects missing markers, conflicting marker values, excessive duplicates, changed transcripts, candidate/authority/rootfs drift, failed temporary boots and any evidence that claims a persistent phone-storage write.

A successful parse records `exact_candidate_identity_bound=true`, `exact_rootfs_identity_bound=true`, `kali_systemd_early_signal_observed=true` and `kali_rootfs_signal_observed=true`.

It deliberately keeps `kali_early_userspace_verified=false`, `manual_review_required=true`, `hardware_verified=false` and `beta_gate_credit=false`.

Only a reviewed physical run on the exact supported phone/firmware can promote this observation into the corresponding Beta-gate evidence.

## What remains unresolved

This contract does **not** decide how the Kali rootfs is transported to or made available on a physical phone. That is a separate hardware/storage design decision and must remain profile-driven. Before the first AC2003 Kali-rootfs boot test, KaliPhoneStudio still needs a safe, explicit and reversible rootfs handoff/staging strategy that is consistent with the exact firmware, UFS/encryption reality, temporary-boot-first policy and recovery path.
